## What it is

### Short

A savings account that pays a much higher interest rate, often 10 to 50 times a traditional bank's rate. Usually offered by online-only banks that have no branches to pay for.

### Long

_Not yet written._

## Who ends up using it

### Short

People holding an emergency fund or saving for something within the next few years who want the money to earn something while staying safe and reachable.

### Long

_Not yet written._

## How it makes money off you

### Short

Same model as any bank, with a smaller cut. The rate is also a marketing tool: it moves with the Federal Reserve's rates and some banks trim it quietly once you've moved money in.

### Long

_Not yet written._

## What goes wrong

### Short

The rate is variable and can drop. Transfers to your main bank can take 1 to 3 business days. Some accounts have tiers or caps on what earns the headline rate.

### Long

_Not yet written._

## What to ask

### Short

Is this FDIC insured? Is the rate the same on the whole balance, or only up to a cap? How long do transfers take? Has this bank's rate held up over the last year?
