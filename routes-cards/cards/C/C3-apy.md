## What it is

### Short

The interest rate is the plain percentage. APY (annual percentage yield) is what you actually earn in a year once interest is paid on interest, called compounding. APY is always equal to or slightly higher than the rate. For borrowing, the equivalent number is APR, which has its own card.

## Who ends up using it

### Short

Anyone comparing two savings accounts or CDs. APY is the number that lets you compare apples to apples.

## How it makes money off you

### Short

Marketing. Ads lead with whichever number looks better. For savings they show APY (bigger); for loans they'd rather you not look at APR at all.

## What goes wrong

### Short

Comparing one account's rate to another's APY and picking wrong. Also mistaking a short promotional APY for the ongoing one.

## What to ask

### Short
