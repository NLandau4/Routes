## What it is

### Short

A bank account built for holding money rather than spending it. No debit card for purchases, usually a limit on how many withdrawals per month, and it pays interest: the bank pays you a small percentage for leaving money there.

## Who ends up using it

### Short

Anyone keeping money aside for a goal or an emergency. Most people's first one is at the same bank as their checking.

_Not yet written._

## How it makes money off you

### Short

The bank lends your deposits out at a much higher rate than it pays you. The FDIC's national average savings rate was 0.38% in August 2026, many large banks paid 0.01%, and competitive online accounts paid around 4%. That gap is the bank's profit.

## What goes wrong

### Short

Earning almost nothing without realizing better options exist. Monthly fees on some accounts if the balance drops.
