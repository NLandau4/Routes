## What it is

### Short

Your employer sends pay electronically into a bank account instead of handing you a check. You give them a routing number and an account number once.

### Short

## How it makes money off you

### Short

Banks love it: a steady deposit is why they offer "$300 bonus with direct deposit" and why fee waivers often depend on it. The bank earns by lending out the money that sits there.
