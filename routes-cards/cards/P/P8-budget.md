## What it is

### Short

A plan for where each paycheck goes before it arrives: bills, saving, spending. Any method works if you look at it monthly. The common ones are a fixed split (needs, wants, saving), giving every dollar a job, or just tracking what left the account last month and deciding what to change.

## Who ends up using it

### Short

Anyone whose money runs out before the month does, and anyone who has no idea what they spent last month. That's most people the first time they try it.

## How it makes money off you

### Short

The idea is free. The apps around it are not: budgeting apps charge monthly fees or sell you the products they show next to your spending. A spreadsheet or your bank's own category view does the same job for nothing.

## What goes wrong

### Short

Making one so detailed it dies in week two. Budgeting on gross pay instead of net. Treating it as a one-time setup instead of a monthly check. Forgetting the once-a-year bills that land all at once.

## What to ask

### Short

What did I actually spend last month, by category? Which of those are fixed and which did I choose? What's the one category I'd change first? When will I look at this again?
