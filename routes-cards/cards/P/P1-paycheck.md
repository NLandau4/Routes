## What it is

### Short

The money an employer pays you for a pay period, after it takes out taxes and anything you've agreed to have deducted. The paper or PDF that comes with it is the pay stub, and it shows every deduction line by line.

_Not yet written._

## Who ends up using it

### Short

Anyone employed by someone else. Self-employed people get paid differently (invoices, no withholding), which is why their taxes work differently too.

_Not yet written._

## How it makes money off you

### Short

It doesn't, but the companies around it do: payroll providers charge your employer, and some "get paid early" apps charge you a fee or tip to advance money you've already earned.

_Not yet written._

## What goes wrong

### Short

Not reading the stub. Errors in withholding, missed hours, or a benefit deduction you never signed up for are common and go unnoticed for months.

## What to ask

### Short

How often am I paid? What does each deduction line mean? Is this a W-2 job (employer withholds taxes) or 1099 work (I owe them myself)?
