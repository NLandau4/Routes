## What it is

### Short

The bank account money moves through: pay comes in, bills and purchases go out. Comes with a debit card. It's built for spending, not for growing money, so most pay little or no interest.

_Not yet written._

## Who ends up using it

### Short

Anyone who gets paid and pays bills. It's the base account most people open first.

### Long

_Not yet written._

## How it makes money off you

### Short

Monthly maintenance fees (often waived with a minimum balance or direct deposit), ATM fees, overdraft fees, and the interest the bank earns lending out your balance while paying you close to nothing.

_Not yet written._

## What goes wrong

### Short

Fees that add up quietly, and keeping a large balance here where it earns nothing while prices rise.

_Not yet written._

## What to ask

### Short

What's the monthly fee and exactly what waives it? Which ATMs are free? What happens if I spend more than I have?
