## What it is

### Short

Cash kept separate from spending money, meant for things like a car repair, a medical bill, or a gap between jobs. It's a concept, not a product: it usually lives in a savings account.

### Long

_Not yet written._

## Who ends up using it

### Short

Anyone whose income could stop or whose expenses could spike, which is nearly everyone. People without one tend to end up on credit cards or overdraft when the surprise comes.

### Long

_Not yet written._

## How it makes money off you

### Short

Nothing. This is one of the few ideas in money with no one selling it. (Banks do benefit when it sits in a low-rate account; see High-yield savings.)

### Long

_Not yet written._

## What goes wrong

### Short

Raiding it for non-emergencies, so it's gone when needed. The opposite risk is keeping far more than needed in cash while it loses buying power to inflation.

### Long

_Not yet written._

## What to ask

### Short

What would I actually need to cover if income stopped? How many months of that feels like enough for my situation? Where can it sit so it's reachable in a day or two but not reachable by my debit card?
