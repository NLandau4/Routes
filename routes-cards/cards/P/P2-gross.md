## What it is

### Short

Gross pay is what you earned before anything is taken out. Net pay is what actually lands in your account. The difference is taxes and deductions.

_Not yet written._

## Who ends up using it

### Short

Everyone who gets a paycheck, and everyone who's ever been surprised that a $25/hour job doesn't put $1,000 in the bank for a 40-hour week.

_Not yet written._

## How it makes money off you

### Short

Nothing directly. But job offers, loan applications, and rent screens all use gross, while your actual budget runs on net. Mixing them up is how people overcommit.

### Long

_Not yet written._

## What goes wrong

### Short

Budgeting on the gross number. For most people net is roughly 70 to 80% of gross, depending on state and benefits.

## What to ask

### Short

What's my net per paycheck? Which deductions are required (taxes) and which did I choose (retirement, insurance)?
