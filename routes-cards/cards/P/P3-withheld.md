## What it is

### Short

Money your employer sends to the government on your behalf out of each paycheck: federal income tax, Social Security (6.2%) and Medicare (1.45%), together called FICA, and state income tax where it applies. The W-4 form you filled out when hired tells them how much to take.

## Who ends up using it

### Short

Every W-2 employee. It's not optional.

## How it makes money off you

### Short

The government doesn't profit, but over-withholding means you lend it money interest-free all year and get it back as a refund. Under-withholding means a bill, sometimes with a penalty, in April.

## What goes wrong

### Short

Treating a big refund as a bonus. It was your money all along. The reverse risk is a surprise tax bill after a second job or a side gig with no withholding.

## What to ask

### Short

What did I put on my W-4, and has my situation changed (marriage, second job, kids)? Does my state have income tax?
